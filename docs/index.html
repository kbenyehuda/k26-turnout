Seat figures divide 120 seats among the three blocs in proportion to their votes. Votes the polls put on lists below the threshold (about 8% of the Netanyahu bloc vote, mostly Winter's list and the Haredi list) are left out; otherwise they ignore the threshold and surplus agreements, so read them as a direction, not a count. Blocs follow 2022 party lines: Yisrael Beiteinu sits with center-left; Ha-Bayit Ha-Yehudi and parties that missed the threshold are "Other".

Turnout: Central Elections Committee, per ballot box, via the open Kolot Nodedim pipeline (each 2026 box matched to its 2022 twin). 2022 split: CEC ballot-box results. Poll scaling: 22:00 exit polls of Kan 11, Channel 12 and Channel 13 (weight 1 each) and Channel 14 (weight 0.3); weighted average: Netanyahu bloc 50.9 · change bloc 51.8 · Arab parties 17.3 · other 0 (the Reservists and the Winter list are below the threshold in all four). The change bloc includes Beyachad (Bennett) and Yisrael Beiteinu. Station feeds lag the national total, so box-level voters are scaled up to the CEC national figure. This is an estimate from turnout only; no votes have been counted.
